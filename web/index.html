A little light goes a long way

Different clues.
One shared signal.

You are the last lighthouse crew on a fogbound coast. Bring home four of five signals before the sea swallows the light.

  1. Gather 2–6 players. Create a room and share the code or invite link. Everyone joins on their own phone or laptop.
  2. Read your private clue. Your scanner shows six possible tiles. Other crew members see a different set. Only one tile appears in every clue.
  3. Talk it through. Use the radio, a call, or the room you're in. Compare coordinates such as A1 and C3.
  4. Vote together. Tap the shared chart. You can change your vote. When every player votes for the same tile, the keeper can lock it in.
  5. Beat the fog. A correct consensus restores a beacon. A wrong answer or a timeout costs a signal. Two misses end the voyage.

The keeper starts each round. A refreshed tab reconnects automatically. A new tab needs to join as a new player. If a player leaves mid-voyage, start a fresh room. Afterglow is a cooperative game with no cash, wagers, or purchases.
